How we work

An operating philosophy
shaped by where we work.

001 — Care reaches first

Minutes, not metrics.

We measure success in seconds saved, not app engagement metrics. We prioritize rapid dispatch over complex features, so we strip away anything that doesn't directly get help to a patient faster. Speed is our primary clinical feature.

  • →Median dispatch time tracked per-product, per-week.
  • →Every PR ships with a "minutes saved" estimate.
  • →On-call rotation includes founders, no exceptions.

002 — Build for the bandwidth we have

Zero-data triage powered by Stella AI.

The patients who need emergency care the most often have the weakest network signals. That’s why our toll-free line connects directly to Stella, our AI prehospital and emergency care assistant. Whether using a feature phone, low-bandwidth 2G, or USSD fallbacks, Stella provides instant, reliable access that never goes offline.

  • →Dial our toll-free line from any phone, even a Nokia 105.
  • →Instant AI voice triage with Stella in English, Yoruba, Igbo, Hausa & Pidgin.
  • →Records sync seamlessly via SMS-relay and USSD when data drops.
003 — Open the rails

Our API is the product.

Precision healthcare begins with accessible infrastructure. We open our GPS dispatch system, health data bank, and B2B safety capabilities as simple APIs so hospitals, insurers, and tech teams can route care in minutes without technical friction.

  • →One-day sandbox approvals for verified developers.
  • →Open changelog, public status page, signed webhooks.
  • →Revenue share, not vendor lock-in.
